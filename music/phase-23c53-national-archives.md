## Australia — National Film and Sound Archive (NFSA)

**Graeme Bell's Dixieland Band — Swanston St Shamble (1944)**

- Primary source: https://www.nfsa.gov.au/collection/item/swanston-st-shamble-graeme-bell
- NFSA identifies the 1944 Melbourne commercial 78-rpm recording, made for William H. Miller's Ampersand label.
- NFSA also presents **Two Day Jag**, another 1944 Bell recording, as an audio excerpt on the same collection page.
- Period: correct. Performer: identified. Country: Australia. Musical genre: authentic Dixieland jazz.
- **Hold:** NFSA's embedded audio/excerpt is not yet a verified redistributable, direct audio file for TOBS. Need exact master/audio URL, whether full track is available, browser playback and rights/permission. Do not scrape or rehost without authorization.

## Phase 23C.54 — Direct archive follow-up (23 November 2026)

**NFSA verified, exact collection pages:**

- [Swanston St Shamble](https://www.nfsa.gov.au/collection/item/swanston-st-shamble-graeme-bell) — NFSA ID **PGB202HA**, recorded January 1944 by Graeme Bell's Dixieland Band. The NFSA page explicitly calls its publicly presented audio an **excerpt**. Recorded on a shellac 78 for the Ampersand label. The archive does not state that the excerpt is licensed for redistribution by TOBS.
- [Two Day Jag](https://www.nfsa.gov.au/collection/item/two-day-jag-graeme-bell) — same collection ID **PGB202HA**, January 1944, also explicitly an **excerpt**, not verified as a complete track or downloadable redistributable master.
- [NFSA Sounds of Australia register](https://www.nfsa.gov.au/stories/deep-dives/sounds-of-australia) documents additional Australian period leads: **Life Without Love** (Frank Coughlan's Trocadero Orchestra, 1937); **The Aeroplane Jelly Song** (Joy King, 1938); **Give A Little Credit to Your Dad** and **Lonesome For Your Mother Dear** (Buddy Williams, 1939); **Digger** (Jack Lumsdaine, 1942); **The Majestic Fanfare** (Queen's Hall Light Orchestra, 1943). These are **historical catalog leads only**, not verified distributable audio files.
