## Australia — National Film and Sound Archive (NFSA)

**Graeme Bell's Dixieland Band — Swanston St Shamble (1944)**

- Primary source: https://www.nfsa.gov.au/collection/item/swanston-st-shamble-graeme-bell
- NFSA identifies the 1944 Melbourne commercial 78-rpm recording, made for William H. Miller's Ampersand label.
- NFSA also presents **Two Day Jag**, another 1944 Bell recording, as an audio excerpt on the same collection page.
- Period: correct. Performer: identified. Country: Australia. Musical genre: authentic Dixieland jazz.
- **Hold:** NFSA's embedded audio/excerpt is not yet a verified redistributable, direct audio file for TOBS. Need exact master/audio URL, whether full track is available, browser playback and rights/permission. Do not scrape or rehost without authorization.

## Canada — Library and Archives Canada (LAC) Virtual Gramophone

The archive describes Canadian 78-rpm and cylinder recordings, including catalog entries with downloadable MP3 audio.

- LAC historical discography overview: https://www.bac-lac.gc.ca/eng/discover/films-videos-sound-recordings/virtual-gramophone/Pages/internet.aspx
- Example confirmed downloadable record: A.J. Boulay, **Canadian set - 3rd change**, circa 1924, Montreal. https://www.bac-lac.gc.ca/eng/discover/films-videos-sound-recordings/virtual-gramophone/Pages/item.aspx?idNumber=1034322713
- Example discographical record: **Quadrille des vieux canadiens**, Isidore Soucy, Donat Lafleur and St. Louis, recorded 30 October 1929. https://www.bac-lac.gc.ca/eng/discover/films-videos-sound-recordings/virtual-gramophone/Pages/Item.aspx?idNumber=1007637609
- These examples verify that the archive contains Canadian traditional music and detailed recording dates, **but neither is a 1936–1945 source**. They are fallback candidates only.
- **Hold:** Identify exact wartime Canadian tracks and inspect download permissions, actual file URLs and playback before adding to manifest.
