# Phase 23C.4 — Major Powers Library Expansion

**Scope:** 10 complete archival recordings added to Albion Home Service, La Voix de Paris, Berliner Tanzorchester, Radio Roma and Radio Krasnaya Zvezda. Two recordings per station; no short samples.

- Source: [LOC National Jukebox 1900–1922 dataset](https://data.labs.loc.gov/jukebox/)
- [Dataset rights statement](https://data.labs.loc.gov/jukebox/README.html)
- [Official individual MD5 checksum manifest](https://data.labs.loc.gov/jukebox/manifest.html)
- Locked sources / individual credits: `music/major-batch2-sources.json`.
- Importer: `music/import_major_batch2.py`. It validates all ten original files before touching playlists, and is idempotent.

**Period note:** These are pre-1923 full historic recordings, chosen to evoke older records heard over a 1936–45-style receiver. Their station affiliation is based on repertoire or sung language, not proof of genuine national radio station broadcasts. The Soviet station includes pre-Soviet Russian traditional and classical repertoire. There is no substitution for Japanese civilian music; the two previously imported tracks remain intact.

**Copyright rule:** deploy only compositions and source recordings documented as public domain or explicitly free for public distribution. Dataset source assumes its historic recordings are U.S. public domain. The original underlying compositions are published before 1923 or traditional as recorded in the dataset. Observe additional jurisdiction-specific rights if distribution scope changes.
