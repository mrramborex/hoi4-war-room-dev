# Phase 23C.51 — Underserved international stations: candidate verification

**Status:** research candidates, not added to playable TOBS. Swedish additions remain frozen. Production unchanged.

## Strong candidate: New Zealand

- **Recording:** The New Zealand Apple Song (1940)
- **Performers:** Theo Walters' Personality Band; possible singer Jean McPherson (unconfirmed)
- **Composer:** Ivan Perrin
- **Date:** 1940
- **Duration:** 2:30; 1.73 MB Ogg Vorbis
- **Source archive:** https://www.ngataonga.org.nz/search-use-collection/search/29134/
- **Wikimedia file:** https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:The_New_Zealand_Apple_Song_(1940).ogg
- **Context:** New Zealand's wartime apple-surplus commercial radio campaign. The file page identifies the specific recording, original performer, and primary archive.
- **Assessment:** authentic period popular radio recording, directly relevant to an underserved station (currently 2 entries, both modern US Navy Band versions of the anthem).
- **Blocking issue:** Commons lists New Zealand public-domain and PD-1996 notices, but explicitly warns the US PD-1996 tag **should not be used for sound recordings**. US recording rights and permission to stream from TOBS are not established.
- **Decision:** **HOLD — research only**, pending confirmation of sound-recording rights or permission from relevant rights holder. File URL appears available on Commons but browser playback on the website has not been tested.
