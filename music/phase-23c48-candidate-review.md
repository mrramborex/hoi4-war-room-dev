# TOBS Phase 23C.48 — Historical Music Candidate Review

Status: research queue only. Nothing in this file is an approved radio track. Production remains unchanged.

## New acceptance process
1. Identify an exact audio recording (performer, title, date) and the actual playable file URL, not merely a disc-label photograph, article, or YouTube link.
2. Verify that the file resolves and that the radio browser can decode/play it. Reject dead links, blocked hotlinks, image files, and unreasonably large sources unless transcoded lawfully.
3. Determine separate rights for composition, performance, recording, and distribution/streaming in the United States. Pre-1972 US sound recordings do not become public domain solely because the composer died long ago or the audio was made in the 1940s. A Commons tag is evidence to review, not final clearance.
4. Require permission or a sound legal basis before public deployment. If permission was granted by the user, record which rights holder, work, recording and use the permission covers.
5. Label recordings that are excerpts, modern covers, alternate transfers, or spoken word. Reject synthetic or misdated audio.
6. Add verified candidates to audio-manifest.json in consolidated batches, preserving attribution and provenance.
