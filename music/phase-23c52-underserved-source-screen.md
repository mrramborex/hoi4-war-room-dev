## Search quality notes

- Searches for 1940s Canadian and Australian song audio frequently return unrelated photographs or music from modern performers; these are not track sources.
- Wikimedia Commons' year-by-country categories are broad historical-media indexes, **not** vetted music catalogs.
- Public-domain tags for film/photographs do not automatically establish rights in separately reused historical sound recordings.
